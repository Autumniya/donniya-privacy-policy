Donniya – User Data Deletion Instructions

Donniya does not use Facebook Login and does not store Facebook profile data.

If you want to delete data related to your use of Donniya, you can follow the instructions below.

1. Delete local app data from your device

Android:

Go to Settings > Apps > Donniya > Storage > Clear data.

iOS:

Delete the Donniya app from your device. This removes local app data stored on the device.

2. Remove Donniya from your Facebook account

If Donniya appears in your Facebook Apps and Websites settings, you can remove it by going to:

Facebook > Settings & privacy > Settings > Apps and Websites > Donniya > Remove.

3. Request data deletion by email

To request data deletion, contact us at:

Email: donniya.kodon@gmail.com

Subject: Donniya Data Deletion Request

We will process deletion requests as soon as possible.
